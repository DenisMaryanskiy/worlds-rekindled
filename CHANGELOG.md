# Changelog

Everything the mod has gained, newest first. Each minor version is one big addition, and its patch
versions are the steps it came in, with the pull requests behind them. Names are today's: the
boss was first called the Watcher, and its lens, trim and gaze were named after it.

The design behind all of it is in [docs/pale-garden.md](docs/pale-garden.md).

## 0.4: More reasons to come back (in progress)

After the third playtest: the fights tuned once more, and rewards for every kind of player.

### 0.4.11 (unreleased)

- **The Elderwood's model** has a chamfered trunk, splayed buttress roots, tapered branches and a
  more distinct crown. The copper collars wrap around the wood, and the lens sits inside its
  mounting. Its rough bark and copper textures, seven hearts and animation pivots are retained.
- **Sentry spawn eggs** now have shaded shells and larger resin and weathered-copper markings.
- **Sentries, the Elderwood and its roots** have connected bark grain instead of pixel noise;
  the Elderwood's copper has small patina patches, and the Sentry statues keep their carved details.
- **The Elderwood painting** now depicts the tree in the woods, framed by the builders' columns,
  with its copper bands, lens and seven hearts.
- **Pale Ruin maps** have a folded parchment icon and a small ruin symbol on the map.

### 0.4.10 (#71)

- **New inventory textures** for the Pale Mask, Creaking Eye, Elderwood Lens, Lens Shard and
  Elderwood armor trim smithing template: hand-authored 16×16 sprites with pale oak grain, amber
  resin, weathered copper and a carved eye on the template.

### 0.4.9 (#68, #70)

The first release, under the MIT license.

- **Translations** into Russian, German, French, Spanish and Italian, using vanilla's own words for
  vanilla things (the Creaking Heart, resin, banner patterns, trims) in each language.

### 0.4.8 (#67)

- **The Elderwood scales for groups**: with more players near the stump when it wakes, up to
  four, every heart takes a crack more per player past the first, and everyone who cracked a
  heart gets a lens of their own.

### 0.4.7 (#65)

- **The Gaze Banner Pattern**, the lens's eye, found in the Pale Ruin's study and hall.
- **The Elderwood**, a painting of the tree and its seven hearts, found only in the hall's chest.

### 0.4.6 (#64)

- **Resin glass** and **resin glass panes**: 8 glass around a block of resin make 8, as dye makes
  stained glass. Translucent amber; like stained glass it needs Silk Touch to pick up, and it tints
  a beacon's beam orange.
- **A resin clump mends a crack** in your Heartwood Sentry, as iron ingots heal an Iron Golem.

### 0.4.5 (#62)

- A Journeyman **Cartographer** can sell a **Pale Ruin Map** for 14 emeralds and a compass, beside
  its other explorer maps. It marks the nearest Pale Ruin with a mark of its own; where there's no
  Ruin in reach the trade doesn't show up.

### 0.4.4 (#61)

- **Tiers**: the copper block on the stump sets the Elderwood's fight. Plain copper is the fight as
  playtested; exposed, weathered and oxidized copper each make it harder (faster roots, resin and
  turning, a crack more on every heart, a faster gaze) and pay more (a second lens, lens shards).
  Waxed copper counts as its stage.
- **One Elderwood Core per tier**, each rimmed in its tier's copper.
- **Verdigris**: a challenge advancement for felling the oxidized tree.
- The Ruin's own stump is now waxed plain copper, so its fight is the lowest tier.
- An Elderwood from a spawn egg or a command is the plain copper tier, and looks it.

### 0.4.3 (#59, #60)

- **The Elderwood Core**, the mod's first block: a trophy the Elderwood drops on every kill. Dull
  resin with a slowly pulsing glow and a faint light; decorative only.
- **Richer chests** in the Pale Ruin: the hall's chest gains 2-3 valuables (iron, gold, emeralds, a
  golden apple, an enchanted diamond pickaxe or sword, rarely an enchanted golden apple) and, two
  times in three, 1-3 diamonds. The tool chest and the store room carry more iron, and now and then
  a diamond or gold.

### 0.4.2 (#57, #58)

- **The lensed Sentry**: an Elderwood Lens used on your own Heartwood Sentry sets itself in its
  chest. Its gaze slows the mob it fights and, held 5 s, roots it in place for 3 s; bosses are only
  slowed. Turned against you, it petrifies you like a Ruin Sentry. Taking it apart or its death
  gives the lens back.
- The plan for the rest of 0.4 written down (in the design doc).

### 0.4.1 (#56)

- Vanilla advancements count the mod: **Monster Hunter** and **Monsters Hunted** count the Ruin
  Sentry and the Elderwood, **Crafting a New Look** the Elderwood trim.
- **Heartwood Guardian**: a new advancement for building a Heartwood Sentry; its description
  spells out how.

### 0.4.0 (#53-#55)

After the third playtest:

- Ruin Sentries break one crack sooner on every difficulty (3, 4 and 5).
- The Elderwood's roots reach as far as its lens sees, so its hall has no safe corners left.
- The roots come for a player it sees at range too, and break a petrified player's stone: standing
  still and hitting its resin back is no longer safe.

## 0.3: The Pale Ruin

An underground temple under the Pale Garden, guarded by Ruin Sentries, with the Elderwood's stump
in its hall. Built, then reworked over two playtests.

### 0.3.4 (#51, #52)

- **The Ruin stays underground**: it only takes a site where ground covers every room, and comes
  with a stairway 20, 40 or 60 blocks down to fit the terrain. Ruins no longer stick out of hillsides.
- Three more kinds of room: the **shrine**, the **foundry** and the **collapsed room**.
- **A mural** of the tree and its seven hearts on the hall's wall.
- **The loot reworked**: each side chamber has its own; the armor trim is always in one heap in the
  hall; a brush is always in the tool chest; one pot of every pair shows a sherd; no amethyst, and
  far fewer pale oak logs.

### 0.3.3 (#48-#50)

- **Side chambers**: every room has a door to a small dead end, a store room, a study, a tomb or a
  collapsed passage, with no Sentries in them.
- **The Sentry workshop**: a chamber that shows how a Heartwood Sentry is built, with the builders'
  own Sentry still standing there.
- **The Guardian sherd**, found on the workshop's pots.

### 0.3.2 (#44-#47)

After the first two playtests:

- The Elderwood's trunk is drawn where it really is, so its hearts are where they look.
- Ruin Sentries: their gazes don't add up, their blow breaks the stone it turned you to, they deal
  less damage, only notice you in their own room, guard their whole Ruin and walk back to their
  post, and don't throw you into the air. A critical hit cracks their heart twice.
- The Elderwood: armor protects against its roots, they come less often, it waits 90 s before it
  mends, it pauses its resin after a stun, and resin you hit back no longer sets you on fire.

### 0.3.1 (#39-#43)

- Bigger rooms, and more kinds of them: guard room, overgrown room, workshop, archive, cells, lens
  room, resin works and nursery.
- **The Ruin's loot**: the hall's chest with a Creaking Heart and lens shards, the tool chest with
  the builders' Silk Touch axe, pots, and suspicious gravel holding sherds and the armor trim.
- **Buried Garden**, an advancement for entering a Pale Ruin.
- The rooms' Creaking Hearts wake as they should.

### 0.3.0 (#35-#38)

- **The Ruin Sentry**: the builders' guard, a Creaking-bodied golem with a petrifying gaze and a
  long reach, hurt only through the heart in its chest. It fears sunlight: in the sun it shields
  its heart and backs away, and blows from a sunlit attacker bounce off.
- **The Pale Ruin**: a pavilion over a stairway, four guard rooms, a second stairway and the hall
  with the Elderwood's stump. Generates in Pale Gardens.

## 0.2: The Elderwood

The boss: the oldest pale oak, woken by a Creaking Heart set back on its stump, with a lens that
turns the Creakings' freeze outward.

### 0.2.4 (#30-#34)

- The Elderwood's feedback polished: chips and shards on its hearts, its death as the tree breaking
  apart, and its own sounds.
- **Five advancements** in the Adventure tab: Old Growth, Set in Stone, Deadwood, One of Them and
  Don't Blink.

### 0.2.3 (#26-#29)

- The branch hearts can be hit where they hang, petrification is easier to survive without a Pale
  Mask (a raised shield blocks the gaze), a weak swing on a heart says why it did nothing, and any
  sword swing cracks a heart.

### 0.2.2 (#20-#25)

- The boss reworked into **the Elderwood**: a rooted tree that grows out of a stump of a pale oak
  log, resin blocks and a copper collar when a Creaking Heart is placed on top. It turns slowly in
  place and fights with **seven hearts** (four in its trunk, one on each main branch and the core
  under its lens), a **branch slam** that leaves the branch planted, **root eruptions** under a
  player it can't see, and a gaze in a cone ahead of it. Left alone it falls dormant and mends.

### 0.2.1 (#17-#19)

- The boss's first version, then called the Watcher, with its summon, its hearts and branch slam,
  and **burning resin** that can be hit back at it to stun it.

### 0.2.0 (#13-#16)

- **The Elderwood Lens**, and its shards (nine make a lens). The Creaking Eye is crafted from a lens
  set in resin.
- **Petrification**: a gaze fills a meter that slows the player down and turns them brittle, then
  petrifies them for a moment. The Pale Mask blocks it.
- Sentry hearts are sturdier, and skeletons' arrows can crack them.

## 0.1: The Heartwood Sentry

The first rewards for the Pale Garden, and a golem to guard your base.

### 0.1.3 (#12)

- A Sentry can be taken apart with sneak and an axe, giving back its heart and its head.
- A jack o'lantern head gives off light.
- The Pale Mask's gaze slows a Sentry, the only thing that does.

### 0.1.2 (#10, #11)

- **The Heartwood Sentry**: built from a pale oak log, a Creaking Heart and a carved pumpkin. A night
  guard that turns to a statue on its pedestal in daylight, fights hostile mobs around it and can
  only be hurt through the heart in its chest, which cracks and heals.

### 0.1.1 (#7-#9)

- **The Pale Mask**: a helmet with a Creaking's face, crafted around a Creaking Eye. Creakings take
  its wearer for one of their own, and its gaze slows the hostile mob it looks at.

### 0.1.0 (#1-#6)

- Sticky pistons can be crafted with a resin clump instead of a slime ball.
- **The Elderwood armor trim** smithing template.
- **The Gaze and Creaking pottery sherds**.
- The design doc for the Pale Garden.

# Pale Garden: playthrough checklist

Reusable checks for the **0.5.0 Pale Garden beta**, from discovering the biome to killing the
Elderwood in survival on Normal. Recent playtests informed the current tuning. Tick what is
fine and note what still needs adjustment, especially with a player who has not read the design.

## Before you start

- `./gradlew runClient`, a new world, survival, Normal. Use only newly generated chunks: Ruins
  that generated before a fix keep their old shape.
- To skip the search: `/locate structure vanillareforged:pale_ruin`. To judge how rare it is, don't.
- Bring what a mid-game player would have: iron or diamond gear, a bow, a shield, food. No
  elytra, no netherite.
- F3 for coordinates when something looks wrong, F2 for a screenshot of it.

## 1. Finding it

- [ ] How long did the Pale Garden take to find, and the Ruin inside it? Did the first garden
      have one at all? (It generates on a 12-chunk grid, so many gardens have none.)
- [ ] The pavilion: does it sit on the ground, or dug in, or on stilts? Is it noticeable among
      the pale oaks, or easy to walk past?
- [ ] **Buried Garden** is granted on the way down.
- [ ] A Journeyman Cartographer's **Pale Ruin Map**: did one offer it, and did the map lead to a
      Ruin? How far was it?

## 2. The rooms

- [ ] Which kinds did you get (guard room, overgrown, workshop, archive, cells, lens room, resin
      works, nursery)? Any kind twice? Does the place read as somewhere research was done?
- [ ] Anything broken: water, lava or a cave cutting through, a wall open to the outside, a
      doorway blocked by furniture, a dark corner with vanilla monsters in it.
- [ ] Is it too dark or too bright to read the rooms?
- [ ] From outside: does anything of the Ruin but the pavilion show, on a hillside, in a ravine,
      under a river or a lake? Fly around it.
- [ ] The stairway is 20, 40 or 60 down depending on the ground. Is the longest too long a way
      back after a death?
- [ ] The new rooms (shrine, foundry, collapsed room): can you get through the collapsed one from
      every door, and do the Sentries find their way?
- [ ] Every room has a side door to a dead end with no guards in it. Did you go in? Did you use
      one to heal or to get out of a gaze, and did it work? Does any door open on bare rock?

**Ruin Sentries** (7 in all: 2, 1, 2, 2)

- [ ] Do you understand, without knowing, that the heart in the chest is the weak point and that
      its eye is what petrifies you?
- [ ] The gaze: 5 s of being watched petrifies, from up to 12 blocks. Too fast, too slow? Do the
      columns and shelves work as cover? Does a shield raised at it work, and feel fair?
- [ ] Being petrified (stuck for 3 s or until a Sentry's blow breaks the stone; that blow lands
      50% harder): frightening, or just annoying? Did it kill you?
- [ ] A blow is 4 damage, about a heart through iron armor. Still too much without a shield?
- [ ] 4 cracks to kill one on Normal, at most one every half second, two for a critical hit.
      How long did one take with a sword, and with a bow? Is the critical hit worth jumping for?
- [ ] Blocking a gaze gives a shield sound and sparks, and grants **Stonewall**. Does its
      advancement hint help you discover the counter?
- [ ] Two at once in a room: manageable? Do Sentries from other rooms still join in?
- [ ] They hunt anywhere in the Ruin, up to the pavilion, and walk back to their post afterwards.
      Is there still a spot to cheese them from? Does one ever stand and watch you hit it?
- [ ] At night: do Creakings walk the rooms that have a heart (second and fourth)? Too many?

**Loot**

- [ ] The tool chest in the second room: is the Silk Touch axe there, and is it obvious what
      it's for? Did you take a heart whole with it?
- [ ] Pots and heaps: worth stopping for with a brush, or noise? Which sherds and how many?
- [ ] The Gaze Banner Pattern and the Elderwood painting: did either turn up, and was it worth
      a second Ruin to find the other?
- [ ] Lens shards by the end: 9 make a lens. How many did you have?

## 3. The hall

- [ ] First look from the stairs: does it land?
- [ ] The chest before the stump: did you open it before the fight? Is it obvious that the
      Creaking Heart in it goes on the stump? If not, what would have told you?
- [ ] The four heaps in the corners: the one by the mural, on its right as you face it, always
      holds the Elderwood armor trim. Did you get it, with the brush from the tool chest?

## 4. The Elderwood

- [ ] Waking it: heart on the copper, in the dark. **Old Growth** granted.
- [ ] Do you work out the fight without reading the doc: only hearts can be hurt, a planted
      branch brings its heart down, its own resin hit back stuns it, the core opens last?
- [ ] The gaze (2 a tick, 3 with the core open, so 2.5 s and under 2 s to petrify), the slam
      (10 damage, 15 on a petrified player), the roots when you hide (6 damage before armor,
      every 4 s), the resin.
      Which of them killed you, and did it feel like your mistake?
- [ ] Do the hall's columns make the fight, or does it turn into running circles?
- [ ] How long was the fight, and how many tries? Does walking away (it mends after 90 s) feel
      right as the way to reset? After a death, was it still as you left it when you got back?
- [ ] Do hits on a heart you can see land every time, also while the trunk turns?
- [ ] Returning resin gives a sound and sparks, and a successful stun grants **Back to Your
      Roots**. Can a first-time player learn this from the hint and feedback?
- [ ] Body hits sound dull and heart hits crack: can you tell which attack counted?
- [ ] Its death: does the staged collapse read, and is the lens where you expect it?
- [ ] **Deadwood** granted; **Set in Stone** if you were petrified and lived; **Don't Blink** if
      you never were.

## 5. After

- [ ] Lens to Creaking Eye to Pale Mask: clear from the recipe book?
- [ ] The mask: Creakings ignore you (**One of Them**), its gaze slows what you look at, it
      blocks petrification. Worth giving up a real helmet for?
- [ ] The Sentry workshop, off the third room: three plinths, a mural, a pumpkin on the bench.
      Did you understand it's a recipe? Did you put the pumpkin on the second plinth's heart?
      Does the builders' own Sentry wandering the chamber help or confuse?
- [ ] A Heartwood Sentry of your own from a heart you took home: built without looking it up?
- [ ] An Elderwood Lens used on your Sentry: can you see it's set? At night, does its gaze slow
      the mob it fights and then root it in place? Is a lensed Sentry worth a lens?
- [ ] The Elderwood Core it drops: does it read as a trophy, placed in your base?
- [ ] With friends: does the tree with a crack more per player feel as long as alone? Did
      everyone who cracked a heart get a lens?
- [ ] After the oxidized fight, try weathered, exposed and finally unoxidized copper collars:
      does each feel harder than the last, and is the extra lens or shards worth it? Is the
      fresh-copper tree beatable, and does it grant **Untarnished**?
- [ ] Fresh copper while wearing the mask: do the quicker slam recovery and resin throws
      still distinguish it from exposed copper, without making the heart openings feel unfair?
- [ ] On Peaceful, trying to wake it gives a message and leaves the entire stump intact.
- [ ] Reconnect with partial petrification and while fully turned to stone: does the state
      survive? Does dying still clear it? Try on a server with another player present.
- [ ] Resin glass: does it look like amber next to orange stained glass? Did you mend your Sentry
      with resin clumps?
- [ ] Is there a reason to come back for a second Elderwood, or a second Ruin?

## Already checked without a player

These ran in a generated world and on a dedicated server, so they don't need watching for:

- A natural Ruin generates in a Pale Garden with all its pieces and its 7 Sentries.
- Buried Garden is granted on entering, and not before.
- Brushing a heap finishes and drops its find.
- The rooms' Creaking Hearts are awake at night and dormant by day, at any depth: vanilla wakes
  them by the time of day alone.
- On a dedicated server with a client connected: petrification reaches the client, the
  Elderwood's cracks sync, and placing a Ruin doesn't break anything.

Not checked by anything but you: whether Creakings actually come out of those hearts with a
survival player near (the checks ran as a spectator, whom hearts ignore), how any of it feels,
and more than one player.

# Worlds Rekindled

Worlds Rekindled gives Minecraft's newer content more reasons to explore, fight, build and return.
It builds on familiar materials and mechanics, with rewards that belong in a vanilla survival world.

**The first expansion brings new life to the Pale Garden.** Beneath its quiet woods lie forgotten
ruins, watchful guardians and an ancient tree bound in copper. Follow their trail, learn how to
face them, and bring a little of their power home.

## Explore the Pale Ruin

Buy a Pale Ruin explorer map from a journeyman cartographer, or discover an overgrown entrance
among the pale oaks. Descend into an underground ruin with varied rooms, archaeology, hidden
treasures and a final hall.

Ruin Sentries guard the corridors with a gaze that can turn you to stone. Use your shield and
the ruin's cover, then aim for their exposed hearts. Creakings stir among the roots at night.

## Wake the Elderwood

Restore the hall's summoning stump to awaken the Elderwood: an enormous copper-bound trunk
with seven hearts, sweeping branches and a petrifying gaze.

Watch its attacks, return its burning resin and strike the hearts when they are vulnerable.
The copper used to summon it changes the challenge: aged, oxidized copper awakens a weaker
Elderwood, while freshly scraped copper brings a harder fight and greater rewards.

## Bring the rewards home

- **Pale Mask:** craft a mask that slows enemies you watch, protects against petrifying gazes
  and lets you walk among Creakings until you provoke them. Its powers wear down its durability;
  resin repairs it.
- **Heartwood Sentry:** build a guardian from a pale oak log, a Creaking Heart and a carved pumpkin.
  It guards its pedestal at night or under cover and rests as a statue in daylight. Fit it with
  an Elderwood Lens to give it a petrifying gaze of its own.
- **New finds for builders and collectors:** Resin Glass, three pottery sherds, the Gaze Banner
  Pattern, an Elderwood armor trim, a painting and four copper-stage Core trophies.
- **Another use for resin:** craft sticky pistons with resin clumps as an alternative to slime balls.

Advancements help introduce the ruin's mechanics and guide you through its challenges.

## Installation

- **Minecraft Java Edition:** 26.3
- **Mod loader:** Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or newer
- **Required dependency:** Fabric API for Minecraft 26.3
- **Java:** 25 or newer

Install Fabric, then place Worlds Rekindled and Fabric API in your instance's `mods` folder.
For multiplayer, install both mods on the server and on every player's client.

Pale Ruins generate in newly explored terrain containing Pale Gardens. In an existing world,
explore new chunks to find them. Back up an existing world before adding the mod.
